About the project

Learning that connects both banks

Know Dublin is an independent educational prototype created by Allies in Action.

Our mission

Know Dublin makes credible introductory learning about the River Liffey and the city’s bridges easier to discover and more engaging to explore. It combines concise reading with a replayable quiz.

Editorial approach

The MVP uses public information from Dublin City Council, the Placenames Database of Ireland and official heritage resources. It has not undergone formal civic or heritage review and does not speak for those organizations. Each learning card and quiz explanation links to its source.

We prioritize official sources for bridge history, engineering, place names and civic context. City-wide and bridge-specific information is clearly labelled. Corrections are welcome through the demonstration contact page.

Project origins

Know Dublin evolved from AllyIndex (opens in a new tab), created by Allies in Action during the Code Institute July 2024 hackathon. It carries forward the learning-through-play concept and the functionality of Know Yellowknife with new Dublin-focused content.
